The core principles of Islamic finance

  • No riba (interest): money should not generate money merely through the passage of time.
  • No gharar (excessive uncertainty) and no pure speculation.
  • Profit and loss sharing: financing is backed by a real asset or a real business activity.
  • Excluded sectors: alcohol, pork, gambling, weapons and similar.
  • Zakat: the third pillar of Islam purifies wealth and is given to the categories of recipients defined in the Qur'an.
