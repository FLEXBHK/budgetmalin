What is compound interest?

When your interest is reinvested, it earns interest of its own. The effect is small at first, then speeds up over time: that is why starting early matters more than the amount you save.

Example: 100 a month for 30 years at 5% grows to about 83,000, while you only paid in 36,000.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

A common target is 20% of your take-home pay (the 50/30/20 rule). If that is too much, start with 5% and increase gradually: consistency matters most.

Should I set up an automatic transfer?

Yes: scheduling a transfer to your savings account on payday is the most effective way to save without thinking about it.

Where should I keep my emergency fund?

In an easy-access savings account, separate from your current account. Aim for 3 to 6 months of essential expenses.
