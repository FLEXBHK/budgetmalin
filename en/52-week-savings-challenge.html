How does the 52-week challenge work?

It's simple: every week of the year, you set aside an amount equal to the week number. Week 1: 1. Week 10: 10. Week 52: 52. Total: 1,378.

The challenge works because the first steps are tiny: the habit forms before the amounts become significant.

Variations

  • Reverse: start with 52 and end with 1. Easier in December, when holiday spending arrives.
  • Small budget: increase by 0.50 a week, for 689 in total.
  • Ambitious: increase by 5 a week, for 6,890 in total.
  • Random: print the chart and colour in whichever amount fits your budget that week.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I keep the challenge money?

In a savings account separate from your current account: still available, no risk, and out of sight so you are not tempted to spend it.

What if I miss a week?

Don't worry: catch up the following week, or use the colour-in chart where you can fill the boxes in any order.
