How does the 50/30/20 rule work?

Popularised by US Senator Elizabeth Warren, this method splits your take-home pay into three buckets:

  • 50% for needs: everything essential (housing, food, utilities, transport, insurance, minimum loan payments).
  • 30% for wants: what makes life enjoyable but you could live without.
  • 20% for savings: emergency fund, future goals, retirement, or paying off debt faster.

If your rent is high, adapt it to 60/20/20 or 70/20/10: what matters is keeping a fixed share for savings.

Frequently asked questions

What if my needs are more than 50%?

That is common in big cities. Start by renegotiating contracts (insurance, energy, phone, internet) and track your spending for a month to find leaks.
